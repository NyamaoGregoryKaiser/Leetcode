             HEAP STRUCTURE (Min-Heap Example)
             ---------------------------------

An array-based representation of a complete binary tree.

Array: [10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60]

Logical Tree:
          10 (idx 0)
         /  \
      20 (idx 1)  30 (idx 2)
     /  \    /
  40 (idx 3) 50 (idx 4) 60 (idx 5)


Heapify-Up Example (Min-Heap): Inserting 5 into [10, 20, 30]
-------------------------------------------------------------

Initial Heap:
      10
     /  \
    20   30

Array: [10, 20, 30]

1. Insert 5 at end:
Array: [10, 20, 30, 5]

Logical Tree:
      10
     /  \
    20   30
   /
  5

2. Heapify-Up (5 vs 20): 5 < 20, swap.
Array: [10, 5, 30, 20]

Logical Tree:
      10
     /  \
    5    30
   /
  20

3. Heapify-Up (5 vs 10): 5 < 10, swap.
Array: [5, 10, 30, 20]

Logical Tree:
      5
     /  \
    10   30
   /
  20

Heap property restored.


Heapify-Down Example (Min-Heap): Pop from [5, 10, 30, 20]
-------------------------------------------------------------

Initial Heap:
      5
     /  \
    10   30
   /
  20

Array: [5, 10, 30, 20]

1. Pop 5. Move last element (20) to root.
Array: [20, 10, 30] (size 3)

Logical Tree:
      20
     /  \
    10   30

2. Heapify-Down (20 vs (10, 30)): 20 is greater than 10. Swap 20 with smaller child (10).
Array: [10, 20, 30]

Logical Tree:
      10
     /  \
    20   30

Heap property restored.


TWO HEAPS FOR MEDIAN FINDER
-----------------------------

`max_heap_low` stores the smaller half of numbers (Max-Heap).
`min_heap_high` stores the larger half of numbers (Min-Heap).

Example `addNum(1), addNum(2), addNum(3)`:

1. `addNum(1)`:
   `max_heap_low`: [1]
   `min_heap_high`: []
   (Balanced: `size(max_low)` = `size(min_high)` + 1)

2. `addNum(2)`:
   `2` > `max_heap_low.top()` (1). Push `2` to `min_heap_high`.
   `max_heap_low`: [1]
   `min_heap_high`: [2]
   (Balanced: `size(max_low)` = `size(min_high)`)

3. `addNum(3)`:
   `3` > `max_heap_low.top()` (1). Push `3` to `min_heap_high`.
   `max_heap_low`: [1]
   `min_heap_high`: [2, 3] (min-heap structure: 2 is top)
   (Unbalanced: `size(min_high)` > `size(max_low)`). Rebalance: Move `min_heap_high.top()` (2) to `max_heap_low`.
   `max_heap_low`: [2, 1] (max-heap structure: 2 is top)
   `min_heap_high`: [3]
   (Balanced: `size(max_low)` = `size(min_high)` + 1)

Median Finder Diagram:

          max_heap_low (Max-Heap)          min_heap_high (Min-Heap)
          ---------------------          ---------------------
          Values <= Median               Values >= Median

          [ ... largest_of_lower_half ... ]    [ ... smallest_of_upper_half ... ]
                     ^                              ^
                    TOP                            TOP
