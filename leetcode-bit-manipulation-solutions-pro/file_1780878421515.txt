This pattern (one '1' followed by all '0's for `n`, and all '0's followed by all '1's up to the position of '1' in `n` for `n-1`) ensures that `n & (n - 1)` will always be 0.

`n = 6` (binary `0110`)
`n - 1 = 5` (binary `0101`)
